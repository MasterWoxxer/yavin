A House of Learning A School for Brilliant Misfits The Anti-Woke Anti-University Join the Rebellion

Universities have failed us, with ideological capture, wildly inflated costs, loans that indenture young people for half their lives, don't prepare them well anymore for careers or entrepreneurship, and long since abandoned any pretense of wisdom or morals on how to live a good life.

Yavin aspires to genuinely prepare students for life in the broadest sense: to orient themselves in place, wisdom traditions, see all the ways humans have lived in history, and learn how to learn and think for themselves. That way they are best prepared for all the challenges- not just careers or business, but leadership, causes, and thoughtful application of their full body and soul to find and choose their mission.

The Way to learn is not the western-school model, but the yeshiva model: hevrutas, core texts, holistic approach that has a certain expectation of personal growth towards next stages of life. Encourages and empowers students to grow so that they become ready to build lives, start businesses, incorporate and transcend the values and structures of their origins and upbringings.

The Study of Human Life: what works and what doesn't work? Community, technology, polity, history. What are goals for human societies large and small? What produces happiness? What choices—of belief, communal forms, technologies, ideologies—lead to what outcomes? What possibilities are there for your community, and humanity, that are genuinely new?

After studying here, the goal is for each student to have developed knowledge and skills to be an entrepreneur, a community builder, a jedi, or a revolutionary… as necessary.
